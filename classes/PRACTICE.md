## Purpose

Each instructional class has a matching ungraded practice module inside its class folder. Practice is not submitted and does not contribute directly to the course grade. It makes concrete the common-core knowledge, calculations, interpretations, and limitations that students should eventually command independently.

## Practice workflow

1. Read the corresponding class guide and its common-core recap.
2. Attempt the recommended practice route before opening the worked solutions.
3. Use staged hints, AI, the textbook, or instructor and TA support when feedback would help, while tracking what you complete independently.
4. Inspect compact checks one problem at a time and investigate discrepancies.
5. Study the worked solution after a genuine attempt, then close it and reconstruct the reasoning.
6. Complete the nearby transfer without assistance and record what still needs practice.

The detailed tutoring, verification, and solution-study protocol is in [`classes/AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md). Every required activity also has a non-AI route.

## Assessment boundary

Practice is ungraded and may use the permitted support above. The four in-class exams are closed-book and unaided. The individual empirical project is governed by its own instructions and permits AI subject to reproducibility, verification, attribution, privacy, interpretation, and individual-responsibility requirements.
