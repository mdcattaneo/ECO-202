## Using a released exam

After release, students may use an exam for timed reconstruction, guided practice, attempt auditing, or deliberate solution study. Begin without the solution, preserve a genuine unaided attempt when simulating exam conditions, and open the released solution only after the attempt or an explicit choice to study it. Historical instructions on a released assessment do not override current course policies.

## Release boundary

An empty folder or placeholder row does not imply that assessment content is available. Questions and solutions remain private until the instructor explicitly authorizes their release after administration. No active assessment assistance is permitted.
