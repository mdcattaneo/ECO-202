## Optional source study

The `.tex` files beside the PDFs are the editable originals. This complete practice folder includes [the local formatting file](exam-style.tex), so it can be downloaded and compiled independently. See [LaTeX build instructions](../../README.md#latex-sources-and-compilation). Reading or compiling sources is optional; it is not an exam skill. Do not open solution sources during a timed attempt.

## Your attempt

Print the paper and set a 70-minute timer. Work without books, notes, calculators, electronics, AI, or solutions, subject to University-authorized accommodations. Fractions, radicals, and equivalent exact expressions are acceptable. Use the reference information provided on the paper and show your reasoning.

After time ends, mark incomplete answers and distinguish questions you understood but could not finish from questions requiring conceptual review. The suggested timing is a practice target, not a grading requirement.

## Review after the attempt

Open the [separate worked solutions](solutions/). Compare one problem at a time, identify the first incorrect or unsupported step, then close the solution and reconstruct the method. Return to the relevant class guide or seek TA/AI help if the underlying idea is still unclear. Finish by explaining the key assumption and interpretation without assistance.

These practice exams illustrate the module’s common core; they are not predictions of the real paper’s questions. See the [exam-study protocol](../../AGENTS.md) for assistance boundaries.
