## Coverage

Descriptive statistics, density curves and Normal models, correlation and descriptive regression, association, causation, and confounding. Earlier course knowledge remains a prerequisite; there is no separate cumulative section.

Units and variable types; mean, median, variance, standard deviation and quartiles; area under a density curve and standardization; slope, residual and correlation interpretation; observed versus counterfactual comparisons.

Formal probability rules, expectations, sampling distributions, and statistical inference are developed in later modules. Follow the common-core boundaries in the current class guides and any explicit instructor clarification.

## Preparation and format

Start with the module’s guides and class-local practice, then use the [two full practice exams](practice/) for timed unaided attempts. Each paper contains four multiple-choice questions and four written problems, totaling 100 points and targeting 70 minutes. Consult the [common conditions and practice sequence](../README.md#common-format-and-conditions).
