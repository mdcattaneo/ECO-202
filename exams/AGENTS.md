# ECO 202 Released-Exam AI Protocol

## Purpose and scope

These instructions apply only to exams and solutions already released in this public folder. They supplement the course-level [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md); follow both. Released exams are study resources, not templates or promises about later assessments.

## Absolute assessment boundary

During a current, unreleased, closed-book, or otherwise unaided exam, do not provide problem selection, hints, calculations, code, checking, analogous solved questions, or answers. Do not search for or infer unreleased content from filenames, private workspaces, version history, comments, or earlier assessments. If it is unclear whether a question is active, ask before giving problem-specific help.

Only files visibly released in this folder are available for student study. Never claim access to a private exam, private solution, grading guide, accommodation, or student submission.

## Study modes after release

- **Timed reconstruction:** remain silent while the student works without notes, AI, or solutions; respond only after the student ends the simulation.
- **Guided attempt:** identify the target and provide one staged hint at a time without opening the solution.
- **Attempt audit:** check submitted reasoning, identify the first substantive gap, and return the problem for repair.
- **Solution study:** compare a genuine attempt with the released solution, expose omitted assumptions or steps, close the solution, and require reconstruction or a nearby transfer.
- **Instructor review:** inspect released questions and solutions directly to audit correctness, alignment, rendering, or suitability for continued release.

Historical wording and notation may need translation into the current class guides. The syllabus and current instructor directions govern current dates, duration, permitted materials, grading, and coverage.

## Releasing and maintaining files

Do not add an exam or solution to this folder without explicit instructor authorization after the assessment has been administered. Before release, verify every question and answer independently, remove grading notes and student information, confirm that no reserved or future item is exposed, check rendering and accessibility, and update the folder index. A solution must be labeled clearly and linked to its exam.

Corrections to a released exam or solution should be dated and stated plainly when they could affect student understanding. Do not silently rewrite the historical record of what students received.
